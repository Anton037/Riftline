# Riftline — current plan

Riftline is a touch-only landscape game for phones and tablets. Keep the painted world, actors,
weapons and sound coherent, warnings readable, and rendering/audio allocation bounded. High detail
is standard, with 30/60/120 FPS caps and 60 FPS by default. The owner merges reviewed pull requests.

## 4.3 powers and the frozen horizon

- Nova, Singularity, Aegis, Event Collapse, Combo Surge and Core portals receive coherent spatial effects.
- Keep player powers in bounded private pools, with unchanged combat and readable enemy warnings.
- Natural world-anchored northern lights, subtle ice reflections and fractured Shelf scenery replace foreground cards and stacked blocks.
- Remove duplicate Nova sound and obstacle transition rings; tune separate discharge, gravity and collapse voices.
- Record the applied prompt, actual review, rendered evidence and measured costs in the
  [power and sky review](POWER-AND-SKY-REVIEW-4.3.md).

## Manual acceptance

Physical iPhone/iPad/Android pacing, heat, speaker balance and long-session behaviour still need device
play. Scripted scenes cannot establish fun or fairness. Full published-release update acceptance stays
with the owner; do not merge or publish automatically.
